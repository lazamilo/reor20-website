01 — The breakthrough

Flood physics, at the speed of a decision.

Conventional hydrodynamic models solve the equations of moving water cell by cell, timestep by timestep. They are trusted — and computationally expensive, so most organisations can only afford a handful of scenarios.

DeepWater is trained on physics. It has learned how water moves across terrain, so the full dynamics of a flood — depth, velocity, extent and timing — arrive in seconds.
